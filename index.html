Got questions?
We’ve got answers.

Do employees need a card?

No. Employees pay by scanning the restaurant’s QR code with the Boosog app.

Where can I use my balance?

At every partner restaurant in the app. Groceries are coming next.

How much does Boosog cost for a company?

You load the meal budgets you choose, plus a small monthly fee per active employee. The fee depends on your team size. Book a demo and we’ll send you a quote.

How long does setup take?

Minutes, once your company is verified. From your dashboard, you add your employees and set their budgets yourself. They download the app and can start using their balance right away.

When do restaurants get paid?

Whenever you want. Request a payout from your dashboard at any time, as long as you have an available balance. Every payment and payout is listed there too.
